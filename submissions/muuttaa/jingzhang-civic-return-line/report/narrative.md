# Formal Narrative

Jing-Zhang Return Line uses Open Track to connect movement, youth public life and civic innovation services. AI is accepted only where an ordinary, accessible and staffed city remains usable before, during and after the pilot.

The 100-day public-value contract keeps six field-performance measures at `not_run`: complete public notice, non-AI equivalent service, accessible-route completion, human takeover, appeal response and site-return acceptance. Three everyday journeys now include twelve offline tabletop failure scenarios. All 12/12 pass protocol-completeness checks, while operational validation remains 0/12. A 24-sample, three-transect field kit remains `field_not_collected`. These are not measured service outcomes. The submission embeds both protocol matrices in `simulation.json`; detailed working CSV, report and rerun script remain in the project evidence archive outside the submission package.
